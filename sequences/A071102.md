## 2. Ready-to-paste comment / annotation

The "fool's diamond of order n" region (Propp, "Enumeration of matchings: problems and progress",
Problem 27) was reverse-engineered from the pixel figure in the primary source
(http://jamespropp.org/matchings.pdf, the order-3 figure) and verified to reproduce all 8
previously published terms exactly: D_n = {(x,y) in Z^2 : |x|+|y| <= n-1} (unit cells), edges =
plain horizontal/vertical grid adjacency (no diagonals) -- planar bipartite; since |D_n| is
always odd the color classes are unequal, so a(n) = sqrt(det(K K^T)) where K is the
Kasteleyn-signed rectangular biadjacency matrix (rows = the smaller color class). a(9)-a(25) were
computed by an exact Kasteleyn-orientation construction (multi-modular CRT determinant) and
independently verified by THREE further lineages, all in exact digit-for-digit agreement: (1) an
independently-coded graph construction using the explicit closed-form square-lattice sign rule
(horizontal edges +1, vertical edges (-1)^x) with a plain Bareiss determinant, anchoring a(1)-a(4);
(2) a second, independently-authored program (different agent, different sign-rule proof, SymPy
exact-integer `domain-ge` determinant) that reproduces the full a(1)-a(8) gate and every new term
a(9)-a(25) exactly; and (3) Seiichi Manyama's 2021 conjectured closed-form trigonometric-product
formula for A071102 (evaluated independently to 200+ decimal digits), which also matches every
new term exactly -- incidentally the first verification that Manyama's conjecture continues to
hold through order 25.
