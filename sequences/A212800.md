## 2. Ready-to-paste comment / annotation

a(12)-a(21) computed via Kirchhoff's Matrix-Tree theorem: the (n,n)-torus grid graph C_n X C_n
(N=n^2 vertices, each connected to its 4 cyclic row/column neighbours) has any Laplacian cofactor
equal to the spanning-tree count; the cofactor was evaluated as an exact big-integer determinant.
Two independently implemented methods were used — fraction-free Gaussian elimination (Bareiss)
and a multi-modular-determinant-plus-CRT reconstruction with an explicit Hadamard-bound
certificate — and they agree digit-for-digit on all ten new terms, both also reproducing every
one of the eleven previously published terms (a(1)..a(11)) exactly, including the n=1,2
multigraph edge-cases. Runtime for a(21) (441 vertices, 440x440 cofactor): a few seconds; peak
RSS under 60 MB for the whole run (both lineages).
